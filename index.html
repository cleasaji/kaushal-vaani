Speak your livelihood. Hear your next skill.

A working prototype of the voice assistant: pick a language, answer a few questions (by voice if your browser supports it, or by typing), and Kaushal Vaani builds a livelihood profile and suggests an NSQF-aligned skill pathway with clear reasons. The production version is planned for toll-free IVR and WhatsApp channels.
